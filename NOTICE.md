# Third-party data notice

ParkAlong includes generated artifacts derived from City of Melbourne open data:

- on-street parking bay sensors;
- sign plates located in each parking zone;
- parking zones linked to street segments;
- historical 2019 parking events.

City of Melbourne data is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Data © City of Melbourne.

ParkAlong also bundles a static Victorian parking catalog generated from anonymously accessible council datasets, official council parking pages, and OpenStreetMap. Each catalog record keeps its source name, link, check time, dataset timestamp where available, and published licence. OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and licensed under the [Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Static catalog records describe locations, restrictions, capacities, or prices only; they do not represent live occupancy.
