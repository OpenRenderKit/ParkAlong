# Code of Conduct

ParkAlong should be a welcoming, practical project for contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make space for questions and different levels of familiarity with Swift, iOS, transport, and open data.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or precise location information without consent.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, even when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sustained hostile conduct are not welcome in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour harms the project or its community. For sensitive conduct reports, use the private reporting path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
